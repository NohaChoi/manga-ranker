          Nothing is ever locked in. Compare now and then re-checks titles it is confident about. One surprising pick only nudges a title; a run of them, or one very big upset (say your #70 beating your #3), makes it uncertain again and Compare finds its new place in a few picks. A title that keeps winning (or losing) more than expected moves in bigger steps until it stops. Rereading a title, or reading a lot more of it since the last import, also reopens it.

          Something looks off?

          Copies a short report to paste into your chat with Claude: app version, settings, counts and recent errors. It never includes your sign-ins or tokens.

          Backup file

          Picks are saved in this browser as you make them. A backup file is the manual way to move them: save it here, open it on the other browser or device with Load backup, and it merges with what is there. Nothing is lost if both sides have picks.
